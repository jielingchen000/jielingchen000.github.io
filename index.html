🌤 WeatherNow

Your personalized local weather forecast. To show accurate weather for your area, this app needs your current location.

Getting Started:
  1. Tap "Get My Location" to allow location access.
  2. Your local forecast will load automatically.
  3. Use get_user_location to retrieve coordinates, then call submitLocation(coords) with the result.

WeatherNow uses your GPS only to show local forecasts. Data is not stored.
